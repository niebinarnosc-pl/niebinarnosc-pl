# Glossary of Terms

This document provides definitions for project-specific terms, domain-specific vocabulary (especially concerning transgender topics to ensure sensitive and accurate language), and any technical jargon used distinctively within the project. This glossary is intended to aid clarity and consistent understanding for all users of the blueprint, including AI agents.

When a term from this glossary is first introduced in a blueprint file, it should ideally be hyperlinked to its definition here.

## Categories

Terms may be informally categorized as:

*   **[Project]**: Terms specific to the project itself.
*   **[Domain]**: Terms related to transgender topics, gender studies, healthcare, etc.
*   **[Tech]**: Technical terms, especially if used in a specific way within this project.
*   **[Blueprint]**: Terms related to the blueprinting process itself.
